## -жь / -жьы

Function:
Expresses old age, antiquity, or past state[cite: 5].

Examples:
лIыжь (old man)[cite: 5]
фызыжь (old woman)[cite: 5]

Source 5


## -гъэ

Function:
Forms abstract nouns with an abstract meaning[cite: 5]. It can also indicate states like childhood or youth[cite: 4].

Examples:
цIыхугъэ (humanity)[cite: 5]
лIыгъэ (courage)[cite: 5]
щIалагъэ (childhood, youth)[cite: 4]

Source:
Source 4, Source 5


## -кIэ (-č'a)

Function:
Forms verbal nouns with a circumstantial meaning, such as the manner of an action[cite: 5]. It also functions as an instrumental case suffix used to create adverbs[cite: 3].

Examples:
жыIэкIэ (manner of speaking)[cite: 5]
махуэкIэ (by day)[cite: 3]
жэкIэ (in flight)[cite: 3]

Source:
Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 3], Source 5


## -гъу / -ġwə

Function:
Forms nouns denoting a participant in an action, a person close by occupation/activity[cite: 5], or members of a group[cite: 3].

Examples:
ныбжьэгъу (friend, comrade)[cite: 5]
гъунэгъу (neighbor)[cite: 5]
q'wāžaġwə (fellow-villager)[cite: 3]

Source:
Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 3], Source 5


## -гъуэ

Function:
Serves to form nouns exclusively from verbal stems with a temporal meaning (time, period)[cite: 5].

Examples:
лэжьыгъуэ (working time)[cite: 5]
къухьэгъуэ (time of sunset)[cite: 5]

Source:
Source 5


## -шхуэ / -šxwa

Function:
Adjectival suffix meaning "great", "big", or "huge"[cite: 3, 4]. 

Examples:
лIышхуэ (big man)[cite: 4]
dəwnay-šxwa (great world)[cite: 3]

Source:
Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 3], Grammar and Transitivity of the Kabardian-Cherkess Language[cite: 4]


## -ншэ / -nša

Function:
Adjectival suffix meaning "being without" or "-less"[cite: 3].

Examples:
āda-nša (fatherless)[cite: 3]
āna-nša (motherless)[cite: 3]

Source:
Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 3]


## -кIуэ

Function:
Forms new nouns primarily from verb stems, denoting a person by their action[cite: 5].

Examples:
тхакIуэ (writer)[cite: 5]
еджакIуэ (student)[cite: 5]
