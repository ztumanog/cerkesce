# Derivational Prefixes

## Derivational Hierarchy Overview (Soviet Academy Standard)

The sequence of derivational prefixes attached to the verb root is governed by a strict 7-order hierarchy (from outermost Slot 7 to innermost Slot 1):

| Order | Prefix Category | Primary Morphemes | Functional Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Order 7 (Outermost)** | Reflexive | зы- / з- | Self-directed action[cite: 1] |
| **Order 6** | Directional | къэ- / нэ- | Spatial orientation (hither/thither)[cite: 1] |
| **Order 5** | Version & Potential | хуэ- / фIэ- | Benefactive version or potentiality[cite: 1] |
| **Order 4** | Comitative / Reciprocal | дэ- / зэ- / здэ- | Joint or reciprocal action[cite: 1] |
| **Order 3** | Locative Preverbs | те-, щIэ-, дэ-, хэ-, и-, блэ-, пы- | Spatial placement (In `003-locative-preverbs.md`)[cite: 1] |
| **Order 2** | Causative | гъэ- | Valence-increasing causation[cite: 1] |
| **Order 1 (Innermost)** | Factitive | у- / гъэ- | Transitive derivation from nominal stems[cite: 1] |

---

## гъэ-

Order:
Order 1 (Factitive) / Order 2 (Causative)[cite: 1]

Function:
Increases the valence of a verb by one argument and converts intransitive verbs into transitive verbs[cite: 1].

Examples:
- кIуэн (to go) -> егъакIуэ (he sends him / makes him go)[cite: 1]
- щIaлэр губгъуэм макIуэ (the boy goes into the field) -> анэм щIaлэр губгъуэм игъакIуэ (the mother sends the boy to the field)[cite: 1]
- е-гъэ-гъэ-къэбзэн (to make someone clean something; outer гъэ- = Order 2 Causative, inner гъэ- = Order 1 Factitive)

Sources:
- Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 1]
- Soviet Academy Grammar (Abitov et al., 1957/1970)

---

## зы-

Order:
Order 7 (Reflexive)[cite: 1]

Function:
Reflexive marker indicating that the verbal action is directed back toward the subject itself[cite: 1].

Examples:
- зы-къы-хэ-дзы-н (to throw oneself into something from hither)
- зэyэжынy -> зэyэжынy жыиIaщ (told her to hit herself)[cite: 1]
- sə-zə-txər (that is writing me down)[cite: 1]

Sources:
- Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 1]

---

## зэ-

Order:
Order 4 (Reciprocal)[cite: 1]

Function:
Reciprocal marker indicating mutual or joint action between participants[cite: 1].

Examples:
- зэ-псэлъэн (to speak with one another / to converse)
- зэ-дэ-лэжьэн (to work together mutually)
- za-/zara- (reciprocal prefix forms)[cite: 1]

Sources:
- Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 1]

---

## хуэ-

Order:
Order 5 (Version / Potential)[cite: 1]

Function:
Benefactive version marker indicating action done on behalf of or for an oblique argument, or potentiality (capability to perform action)[cite: 1].

Examples:
- сэ эр зыхуэсшэр (the one who I am leading him for)[cite: 1]
- сэ эр лIыжьым хуызошэ (I lead him for the old man)[cite: 1]
- хуэ-кӏуэн (to go for someone / something)
- xwə- (potential version prefix)[cite: 1]

Sources:
- Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 1]

---

## дэ-

Order:
Order 4 (Comitative)[cite: 1]

Function:
Comitative prefix denoting joint action or association with another entity[cite: 1].

Examples:
- дэ-кӏуэн (to go along with someone)
- zdə- / zəda- (together / comitative marker)[cite: 1]

Sources:
- Matasović: A Short Grammar of Kabardian[cite: 1]

---

## ӀэщӀэ-

Order:
Involuntative Operator[cite: 1]

Function:
Involuntative prefix indicating an accidental, unintentional, or non-volitional action[cite: 1].

Examples:
- ?aś'a- (involuntative verb prefix)[cite: 1]
