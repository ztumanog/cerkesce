# 006 - Word Families (Leksik Kök Aileleri Kayıt Defteri)

## Purpose & Definition
A word family consists of a core lexeme and all derived lexical descendants sharing a common semantic origin. It strictly excludes locative preverbs (Layer 003), TAM operators (Layer 004), and personal agreement markers (Layer 004).

---

## Registry Rules

### Inclusion Rule
Only lexical descendants (new noun/adjective/verb base forms created by compounding or derivational suffixes) are included.
* *Example:* `щхьэ` → `щхьэуз` (Valid Lexeme)

### Exclusion Rules
* **Locative Preverbs (Layer 003):** Locative and directional preverbs (`щы-`, `къэ-`, `нэ-`, `те-`, `дэ-`, `бгъэдэ-`) do NOT create word families.
* **Verbal TAM Operators (Layer 004):** Tense, Aspect, Mood markers (`-а`, `-н`, `-т`, `-щ`, `-къым`) belong strictly to inflectional morphology.
* **Personal Agreement Markers:** Subject/Object markers (`сы-`, `у-`, `ды-`, `фы-`) do NOT constitute lexical derivation.

## Family 001 - гу

### Core Meaning
- heart
- inner self
- center

### Semantic Expansion
HEART
↓
FEELING
↓
THOUGHT
↓
CHARACTER
↓
MORALITY

### Derived Forms
* **`бзэгу`** → language / talkative (literal: speech-heart)
* **`мафӀэгу`** → train (literal: fire-heart)
* **`гуфӀэ`** → joy (literal: good-heart)
* **`губзыгъэ`** → intelligence (literal: sharp-heart)
* **`гуэтыныгъэ`** → loyalty (literal: giving-one's-heart)
* **`губжьы`** → anger
* **`гужьей`** → anxiety

### Metaphorical Logic
heart ↓ emotion ↓ thought ↓ ethics
